Data Analytics · Wellness Tech

Bellabeat Leaf: Smart Device Usage Analysis

Analyzed FitBit data across 33 users for Bellabeat, a women's wellness tech company, to surface behavioral trends and inform marketing strategy. Worked the full analytics cycle — Excel for cleaning, BigQuery (SQL) for querying, Tableau for visualization. Central finding: sleep quality issues cut across all activity levels equally, making sleep the most universally relevant hook for the Bellabeat Leaf. Delivered three data-backed recommendations covering personalized app content, a gamified steps challenge, and behavior-triggered notifications.

Dataset33 FitBit Users
ToolsExcel, BigQuery, Tableau
Key FindingSleep = universal hook
